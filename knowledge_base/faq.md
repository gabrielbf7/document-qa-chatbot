# Maplewood Dental Studio — Frequently Asked Questions

## Are you accepting new patients?
Yes. We welcome new patients of all ages, including children from their first birthday. See the New Patient Guide for what to bring to your first visit.

## Where are you located?
418 Cedar Ridge Avenue, Suite 200, Ashford Falls, OR 97321. We are on the second floor of the Cedar Ridge Professional Building, with elevator access.

## What are your opening hours?
Monday to Thursday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Friday, 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Saturday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM on the first and third Saturday of each month only. Closed on Sundays.

## How do I book an appointment?
You can book by phone at (541) 555-0148, through the online patient portal at maplewooddental.example/book, or in person at the front desk.

## How much is a routine cleaning?
A routine adult cleaning costs $110. A child cleaning (under 14) costs $85. See Services and Prices for the full price list.

## How much does a first visit cost?
The new patient exam, including a full set of X-rays, costs $145. If you need a cleaning on the same day, it is charged separately.

## Do you accept my insurance?
We are in-network with BlueHarbor Dental PPO, Summit Shield Dental, Evergreen Mutual Dental and Northstar Dental Plan. For other plans, we submit the claim for you as a courtesy, but payment is due at the time of the visit. See Insurance and Payment for details.

## I don't have insurance. Do you have a discount plan?
Yes. The Maplewood Smile Club costs $29 per month or $299 per year and includes two cleanings, two exams, annual X-rays and 15% off other treatments.

## Can I pay in installments?
Treatments over $600 can be split into 3 or 6 interest-free monthly payments, subject to approval. Third-party financing is also available through CareBridge Financing for up to 12 months.

## What is your cancellation policy?
Please give at least 48 hours' notice. Late cancellations cost $25 and missed appointments without notice cost $40. See the Booking and Cancellation Policy.

## Do you see dental emergencies?
Yes. We keep two same-day emergency slots every weekday, at 10:30 AM and 3:30 PM. An emergency visit costs $120, which is credited toward treatment if it is done on the same day. After hours, call our on-call line at (541) 555-0199.

## Do you treat children?
Yes. Dr. Priya Nair is our pediatric dentist. We recommend a first visit by age 1. Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian at their first visit.

## Do you offer teeth whitening?
Yes. In-office whitening costs $450 and a take-home whitening kit costs $260.

## Is there parking?
Yes. Free parking is available in the building garage, levels P1 and P2. Bring your ticket to the front desk for validation (up to 2 hours).

## Which languages does your team speak?
English. Our hygienist Sofia Martins also speaks Spanish and Portuguese.

## Can I get a copy of my dental records?
Yes. Ask at the front desk or email privacy@maplewooddental.example. Electronic copies are free and are sent within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy.
