MAPLEWOOD DENTAL STUDIO - FRONT DESK MANUAL (INTERNAL)
Version 3.2 - updated March 2026

1. GREETING
- Answer the phone within 3 rings: "Maplewood Dental Studio, this is [name], how can I help you?"
- Greet walk-in patients right away, even if you are on the phone (a smile and a nod).
- Use the patient's name. Confirm date of birth before discussing any appointment or record.

2. WHAT THE FRONT DESK CAN ANSWER
- Hours, location, parking, holidays.
- Prices from the official price list (Services and Prices).
- Insurance plans we accept and payment options.
- Booking, rescheduling, cancellation and fees.
- What to bring to the first visit.

3. WHAT THE FRONT DESK MUST NOT ANSWER
- Diagnosis or treatment advice ("Do I need a root canal?").
- Medication questions: which pain reliever to take, doses, drug interactions,
  or whether a medicine is safe. Always transfer to a dentist.
- Final cost of a treatment plan: give the list price only and offer a written estimate.
- Insurance coverage percentages: offer to verify with the insurance company (1-2 business days).

4. WHEN TO TRANSFER TO A HUMAN SPECIALIST
- Clinical or medication questions -> the dentist on duty.
- Billing disputes, refunds, payment plans -> Rita Alvarez, Office Manager.
- Complaints -> Rita Alvarez. Write down the complaint and promise a call back within 1 business day.
- Insurance verification -> Daniel Cho, Patient Coordinator.

5. EMERGENCIES
- Life-threatening signs (trouble breathing or swallowing, swelling spreading to the eye or neck,
  heavy bleeding that does not stop, high fever with facial swelling, injury after an accident):
  tell the patient to call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room immediately.
- Dental emergencies during opening hours (severe toothache, broken tooth, lost crown,
  knocked-out tooth): offer the same-day slots at 10:30 AM or 3:30 PM.
  Knocked-out tooth: tell the patient to come immediately, keeping the tooth in milk.
- After hours: give the on-call line (541) 555-0199.

6. PRIVACY
- Never discuss a patient's information with anyone who is not the patient, parent/guardian of a minor,
  or a person authorized in writing.
- Do not leave details of treatment in voicemail. Leave only name and phone number.
- Do not ask patients to send medical information by text message, chat or social media.

7. TONE
- Friendly, calm and clear. Avoid dental jargon.
- If you do not know an answer, say so and offer to find out. Never guess.
