MAPLEWOOD DENTAL STUDIO - AFTERCARE INSTRUCTIONS

These are general instructions. Always follow the specific instructions your dentist gave you.
For questions about medication, call the office and speak to a dentist.

AFTER A CLEANING
- Mild gum sensitivity for 1-2 days is normal.
- If you had fluoride applied, wait 30 minutes before eating or drinking.
- After a deep cleaning, avoid very hot, spicy or crunchy food for 48 hours.

AFTER A FILLING
- If you had numbing, wait until it wears off (usually 2-3 hours) before eating, to avoid biting your cheek.
- Some sensitivity to cold is normal for up to 2 weeks.
- If your bite feels "high" after the numbing wears off, call us for a quick adjustment (no charge).

AFTER TEETH WHITENING
- Avoid coffee, tea, red wine, cola, berries and tobacco for 48 hours.
- Temporary sensitivity is common for 1-3 days. Use a toothpaste for sensitive teeth.
- Results usually last 1 to 3 years, depending on habits.

AFTER AN EXTRACTION
- Bite firmly on the gauze for 30 to 45 minutes.
- Do not rinse, spit forcefully or use a straw for 72 hours.
- Do not smoke or vape for at least 72 hours.
- Eat soft, cool food on the first day (yogurt, soup that is not hot, mashed potatoes).
- Use a cold pack on the face, 15 minutes on and 15 minutes off, during the first day.
- Start gentle warm salt-water rinses 24 hours after the extraction.

CALL THE OFFICE IF
- Bleeding continues for more than 24 hours.
- Pain gets worse after day 3 instead of better.
- You have a fever or swelling that keeps increasing.
