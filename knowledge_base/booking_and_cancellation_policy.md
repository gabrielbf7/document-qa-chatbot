# Maplewood Dental Studio — Booking and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026

## How to book
- By phone: (541) 555-0148, during opening hours.
- Online: patient portal at maplewooddental.example/book, available 24/7.
- In person: at the front desk, Suite 200.

New patients should allow 60 to 75 minutes for the first visit. Routine cleanings usually take 45 minutes.

## Appointment reminders
We send a text message reminder 72 hours before each appointment. Reply **C** to confirm or **R** to request a new time. Unconfirmed appointments are still kept on the schedule.

## Late arrivals
If you arrive 15 minutes or more after your appointment time, we may need to shorten the visit or reschedule it, so that other patients are not delayed. A late arrival that must be rescheduled is treated as a late cancellation.

## Cancellations and rescheduling
- Please cancel or reschedule at least **48 hours** before your appointment.
- Cancellations with less than 48 hours' notice: **$25 late cancellation fee**.
- Missed appointments with no notice (no-show): **$40 no-show fee**.
- Fees are waived once per calendar year, and always in case of documented illness or family emergency.

## Repeated no-shows
Patients with three no-shows in a 12-month period will be asked to pay a **$50 deposit** when booking future appointments. The deposit is applied to the cost of the visit.

## Waitlist
If you want an earlier appointment, ask to join the waitlist. We contact waitlist patients by text message when a slot opens up, in order of request.

## Minors
Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian at their first visit. For later routine visits, a signed consent form allows patients aged 14 to 17 to attend alone.

## Emergency appointments
Two same-day emergency slots are reserved every weekday (10:30 AM and 3:30 PM). They are booked by phone only, first come, first served.
